# Runway: the startup game

A 60-second browser game about the one number that kills startups: cash runway.

You start with an idea and six months of money. Six decisions follow, each a real trade-off founders face: build the full product or test an MVP with ten customers, interview people about past behaviour or send a survey, undercut a competitor or price for profit, take the big order or the small ones that pay today, accept investor money with growth conditions, and what to cut when a crisis halves demand. Every choice adds or removes months of runway and ends with a one-line lesson (an MVP is the cheapest way to learn, profit and cash are different things, capital buys time).

Play it: https://davidthestudent.github.io/runway_game/

## Why

I wanted a tiny, shareable way to explain unit economics and cash flow to non-founders without a deck. Everything is in one `index.html`: the scenarios are a small array of objects, the state is a handful of numbers, no framework, no build step.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. That is all.
